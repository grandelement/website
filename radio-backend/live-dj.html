How the stem buttons work
A normal song file can play immediately as FULL. For true DRUMS, BASS, HARMONIC or VOCALS control, load the matching stem file into that deck. Once any stem is turned on, FULL is muted and the selected stems play together in sync. VOCAL CUT works immediately on a normal stereo song by cancelling centered material; it is an effect, not true AI separation.
