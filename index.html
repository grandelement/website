STORY / UNIVERSE VIEWING: Normal mode is the free-form Grand Element universe. Change the dimensional background, move through the scene, interact with the Sun, planets, Bodies, souls/comets, hidden text and music without having to play the arcade game.

  • Hidden text: Appears near cursor, and randomly on its own; click to pin/unpin.

9 BALL RULES

9 Ball: contact the lowest numbered planet first. Pocket a ball legally and you continue. Hit the correct planet but pocket nothing and the turn passes. A wrong first contact, scratch, or no rail after contact is a foul and gives ball in hand. A legal 9 wins. Double tap the Blue Sun to move it when ball in hand.

PLANETS

Throw only. Knock your own album planets beyond the ring for points. The larger shooter is the taw. If you score and the taw stays in the ring, you shoot again from where it stopped. A miss or taw leaving the ring ends the turn; target planets that leave after the taw still count. Tracers show each throw path.
