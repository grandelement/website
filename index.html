BACKGROUND CONTROLS
STORY / UNIVERSE VIEWING: Normal mode is the free-form Grand Element universe. Change the dimensional background, move through the scene, interact with the Sun, planets, ships, souls/comets, hidden text and music without having to play the arcade game.
  • Blue Sun: "Click" Teleport to another dimension. Move wherever you want.
  • Album Planets: Shadows under current song playing. "Click" for album link. Drag/throw for pool-ball physics.
  • GE Soul particles (Comets): Continuous random flow. Click and throw. Similar apparent sizes can enter quantum reactions; rare souls have larger interaction fields.

The first captured soul can settle in the ship center while additional souls orbit outside. Press and hold the thin outer ring to keep the ray on and sweep it through 360°; hold in the surrounding field to steer.
  • Hidden text: Appears near cursor, and randomly on its own; click to pin/unpin.
